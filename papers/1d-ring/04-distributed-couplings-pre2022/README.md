## 1. Paper Reference
- **Title**: Swarmalators on a ring with distributed couplings
- **Journal**: *Physical Review E* 105, 064208 (2022)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Couplings $J_i, K_i$ drawn from statistical distributions rather than uniform constants.
- Study of disorder and frustrative competition between attractive and repulsive swarming interactions.
