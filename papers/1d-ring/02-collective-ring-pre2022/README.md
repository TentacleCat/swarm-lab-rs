# Collective behavior of swarmalators on a ring (Phys. Rev. E 2022)

---

## 1. Paper Reference
- **Title**: Collective behavior of swarmalators on a ring
- **Authors**: Kevin P. O'Keeffe et al.
- **Journal**: *Physical Review E* 105, 014211 (2022)
- **DOI**: [10.1103/PhysRevE.105.014211](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.105.014211)
- **Upstream Code**: [Khev/swarmalators/1D/on-ring](https://github.com/Khev/swarmalators/tree/master/1D/on-ring)

---

## 2. Key Contributions
- Rigorous Ott-Antonsen ansatz application and continuity equations in the limit $N \to \infty$.
- Theoretical bifurcation boundaries between Sync, Async, Phase Wave, and Chimeras on the 1D ring.
- Investigation of finite-size effects and stability diagrams.
